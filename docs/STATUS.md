## Open docket

### qBLC and Object B

The ratified architecture and staging history are in
`docs/quantum/architecture.md`. The two target objects are the operator census
M_Fock (Tr = Ω_success, number-superselected) and the dimension-conditioned
Gács family G_k. The signature order is frozen: `p h meas new cnot t`.

The engines are `src/qeval.rs` (naive executable specification), `src/dw.rs`
(exact ring), and `src/qvm.rs` (KN-store fast path, about 200× naive and
lockstep-verified against qeval over the full ≤24 population). The relevant
drivers are `qcensus` (`--cond-k K` is Object B mode `p k̄ ⟨sig⟩`), `qpilot`,
`qselfint`, `qradical`, and `qcomplement`.

Canonical `data/quantum/census_table.txt` covers the full 526,039,969-program
population 4..41 (~30 min) at β=4096/trans=2²⁶. β×16 resolves zero unknowns;
the 1,619,650-term unknown column is a real frontier.
Ω_{success,≤41} = 3424188513/2⁴⁰; M^(1) is positive definite, ranking
|0⟩ ≫ |+⟩ > T|+⟩ > |−⟩ ≫ |1⟩. The first entangled halts occur at exactly
n=41 (|00⟩ ≫ Φ⁺ > Φ⁻ > |++⟩). Halt masses are dyadic through 41 while
operator entries are irrational; √2-parts cancel in every trace.

Self-interpretation is E_q = `intL I` = **176 bits**. HOAS collapses the
adapter to 6 bits; this is tight within the `intL` protocol, while global
optimality remains open. Effect-trace equivalence is verified; the proof
obligation and plan are in `docs/quantum/bisimulation.md`.

The idiom-sector Σ_success becomes non-dyadic at exactly **n=53**, through the
unique fate-divergent witness P53 (pinned test; n=53 unknowns are
β-insensitive). The phase-2 `qcomplement` campaign sweeps the non-λ⁵
complement at β=512/trans=2²⁰, streaming unresolved terms to `.phase2/` in
≤1–2 hour chunks. Its √2 coefficient is exactly zero at every size 42..51,
with fatediv zero, so full-population dyadicity stands through 51. The n=51
row confirmed the pre-registered wrapped-witness45 orbit: 12 programs and 24
σ-paired leaves. The witness zoo is in `docs/quantum/galois.md` §3, every
witness wire is an `src/odd.rs` fixture, and per-size detail is in the ledger.

The campaign is paused at 51. Remaining when resumed: n=52 in about two
`i/m` slices and n=53 in about four. The big adjudication is deferred to its

### √2 theorem lane

Active. The Tier-A accounting identity and T1 finite-trace Galois twist are
proved/ratified, with Δ(C) for limits; P53 is witness45 plus an 8-bit split;
and n_{1/3} ≤ 85. The rational-limit threshold is a separate problem.

Stage 1a is scoped CNOT-free. The minimum CNOT-trace weight lies in (22, 28],
so a latch-based bound caps at 28; the CNOT sector belongs to stage 1b via
Pauli-string path parity (`docs/quantum/galois.md` §4). The instruments are
`src/odd.rs`, the trusted trace monitor, and `src/oddmin.rs` plus
`oddminproto`, the reference compositional DP specified in
`docs/quantum/oddmin.md`.

Current measurement: witness45 accepts closed with a 44-node summary;
cnot28 rejects; the abstraction is exact against qeval on all 6,069 closed
programs ≤22, with 19 ⊤ cells all concretely non-odd (the alpha-only
port-identity canonicalization loss); zero splice-⊤ through W=24; closed-slice
counts 96/751/6,346 at W=16/20/24; driver 1.1 s. Growth is about ×1.7/bit and
crosses the 10⁶ stop near W≈34.

Next, in order: BindId alpha-normalization plus weak-ε canonicalization and
port renumbering; rerun 24 and probe 26/28/30; simulation-preorder antichain
after constructor-monotonicity checks; the general component-scoped
post-fixpoint with ScopeId origins and a checker that verifies the
post-fixpoint only; then search-side pruning for the ladder to 44. The
handle-aliasing lemma is closed, pre-CNOT only; CNOT's Church pair re-enables
aliasing in stage 1b.
